A typical Compressed Bio-Gas plant in India goes online — both physically and on the web — at the same moment. Ribbon cutting, branded site launches, first dispatch slip, all in the same week.

We've gone the other way. The plant in Thatipadu, Vizianagaram is still in pre-construction. Land is bought, technology partner is selected, IOCL Letter of Intent is signed, term loan is structured. Nothing else has been built yet.

So why publish a site now?

## Because the public record is part of the plant

A CBG plant is not just a digester and a compressor. It is a 50-kilometre web of farmer relationships, payment-discipline, supply-slip integrity, gas-quality verification, FOM offtake, and lender confidence. None of that infrastructure exists in steel. All of it exists in trust — and trust, in our experience, is built by **publishing what you promise long before you have to**.

We've made four promises on the [about page](/about/):

- We use the tech that already works.
- We pay farmers within 14 days.
- We measure what we promise.
- We start small and finish what we start.

Each of those is testable. The only way to make them stick is to start the record before there's anything photogenic to brag about.

## What we will publish, starting the day concrete is poured

One post per month. Each one:

1. **A photo from the site.** No staging. No drone shots until they're useful.
2. **One milestone hit and one missed.** Both. Without missed milestones, the post is useless.
3. **One financial number.** Cash position, drawdown stage, capex spent — one number, named.
4. **One thing that went wrong.** Construction has surprises. We'll publish ours.

This is not a marketing schedule. It is the trust mechanic that pays back when, two years from now, a banker, a farmer, an IOCL procurement officer, or a journalist wants to know whether we did what we said we'd do.

## What this site won't pretend

- We don't have a portfolio of plants. We have one. The about page says so.
- We don't have founder photos yet — Suresh and Deepika are real people who haven't sat for a portrait. The avatars on /about/ are placeholders.
- The founder quotes carry a `[Draft — pending founder approval]` chip until they're signed off.
- The Telugu version of [/farmers/](/farmers/) is in the works for 2026 Q3.

None of that is hidden. All of it is fixable.
