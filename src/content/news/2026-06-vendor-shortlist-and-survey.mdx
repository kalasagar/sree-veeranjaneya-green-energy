A short month-end update from the pre-construction phase.

## What we did

- **Equipment shortlist down to three vendors.** Raj Process Equipments & Systems (Pune) remains the integrator. Quotations are in for the digester, the 2-tower VPSA, and the dispatch cascade.
- **Topographic survey complete on the Thatipadu plot.** The 2.63 acres slope west to east at about 0.4%. Civil concept layout updated; the digester pad and the FOM-separator pad anchor against the natural fall.
- **Paddy-straw price benchmark.** Talked to 23 farmers and two aggregators across a 25 km radius. Working delivered-to-gate price came in at ₹2,100/tonne for dry baled straw, slightly above the indicative DPR figure. We'll publish the first signed supply rate when the first slip is issued.

## What didn't get done

- The PESO Online application is still in clarification with our consultant. We had hoped to file by the end of June. Now targeted for mid-July.
- The Telugu version of the [Farmers](/farmers/) page is still in translation. Aiming for Q3 to coincide with the first pickup-route announcement.

## One operational number

Paddy-straw working price across the 25 km radius came in at **₹2,100/tonne** delivered-to-gate. The farmer-side rate sheet on [/farmers/](/farmers/) will be updated when the first numbered supply slip is signed.

## One thing that went wrong

Two of the three CSTR digester quotations came back about 6% above the original DPR allowance. Investigating whether this reflects a real input-cost movement or a bid-bunching artefact. If it's real, the variance gets absorbed against the contingency reserve — capital structure unchanged.
